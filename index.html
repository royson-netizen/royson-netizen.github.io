How Kigo Works

Simple workflow from customer inquiry to completed delivery

The Process

  1. Customer Chats: They click the link and Kigo activates instantly.
  2. Agent Qualifies: Kigo gets the location and order details.
  3. Quote Generated: A professional invoice is sent instantly.
  4. Dispatch: You approve, and a rider is assigned.
  5. Completion: Payment is reconciled via M-Pesa.
