The Technical Pipeline

How we bypass expensive Meta APIs to deliver unlimited AI automation.

1

WebSocket Connection

We use Baileys (Node.js) to connect Kigo as a "Linked Device", entirely bypassing the restrictive Meta API per-message taxes.

2

LLM Processing

Messages hit ultra-low latency models (Gemini 1.5 / Groq Llama-3) equipped with custom prompts to understand Kenyan context and slang.

3

Make.com Routing

The AI determines the intent (e.g., "Make a payment" or "Log a complaint") and triggers live Make.com webhooks to act.

4

Live Execution

Whether it's pushing a Daraja STK prompt, updating a Google Sheet, or generating a video script, Kigo executes and replies instantly.
