The Architecture of Resilience

In February, we presented the first bare-metal WhatsApp AI prototype in Kenya at C4DLab. While others were building basic conversational wrappers, we realized the street economy didn't need a chatbot or complex architecture. It needed financial infrastructure.

We went heads down. We upgraded the underlying architecture, moved to custom WebSockets on Google Cloud to bypass API overhead, and evolved from a simple bot into a complete Agentic OS. Kigo became Kiggo.

We stopped being a bot and became a bankable operating system.

The Kiggo Ecosystem

Zero behavioral change. We bring the operating system to where the users already live.

Kiggo OS

A 24/7 AI sales agent that bargains in Sheng and Swahili. It checks inventory, negotiates prices within your set margins, and closes sales while you sleep.

Kiggo Dispatch

Hyper-local peer-to-peer logistics. Kiggo drops order tickets into verified stage WhatsApp groups. The first rider to reply #claim gets the delivery.

Kiggo Ledger

Making the unbankable, bankable. Every M-Pesa transaction is silently logged into a secure Google Sheet, giving vendors an instant, verifiable credit profile.
