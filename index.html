How it works

Connect your WhatsApp number, set your delivery zones and templates. Kigo handles the chat and posts events to your dashboard.

1. Connect WhatsApp

Use a WhatsApp Business number or a verified provider. We guide the setup and webhooks.

2. Configure flows

Set prompts for quotes, order intake and tracking. Drop-in templates get you live fast.

3. Go live

Share your number. Customers chat; Kigo responds and your team monitors exceptions.

4. Learn and improve

Dashboards surface volumes, repeat questions and delivery performance so you can tune operations.
