The 3 Bottlenecks of Informal Commerce

Why MSMEs struggle to scale in the current digital landscape.

01. Sales Paralysis

Delayed WhatsApp replies cause drop-offs. High-ticket businesses lose sales because they can't juggle walk-ins and chat inquiries simultaneously.

02. Logistics Nightmare

Fulfillment is a high-friction mess. Manual phone calls to find reliable riders erode margins and frustrate buyers with slow delivery.

03. Data Blackout

Transactions live in unstructured text. Without a verifiable P&L, MSMEs remain unbankable, locking up KShs 2.4 Trillion in credit.
